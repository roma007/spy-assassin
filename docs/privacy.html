我们收集什么

零收集。本应用无需注册账号、不含广告 SDK、不接入任何统计或第三方追踪服务，不使用云服务。所有检测数据（图像、网络扫描结果、传感器读数）均在您的设备本地内存中处理，不会上传或保存到服务器。

数据如何存储

本应用不落盘、不存储任何图像与扫描数据。您主动标记的“我的设备”列表仅保存在本机（应用内部存储），用于下次扫描时排除已知设备。卸载应用或清除数据后即彻底删除。

权限用途

相机：红外检测与镜头反光扫描，仅在您进入检测页时开启，图像仅在本地实时分析，绝不记录或上传。
本地网络：WiFi 扫描需访问当前局域网中的设备，结果仅在本地显示。
定位（Android）：系统要求定位权限才能读取 WiFi 信息，不会获取您的精确位置，也不会上传位置。
蓝牙：仅用于可选的蓝牙设备扫描，扫描结果在本地显示。

检测结果的说明

本应用的检测结果仅供参考，不构成任何法律证据。网络扫描只能发现当前 WiFi 下联网的设备，离线或使用独立供电存储的摄像头无法被检测到。请结合人工排查使用。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向儿童设计，也不收集任何儿童个人信息。

联系方式

如对隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过应用商店的开发者联系信息与我们联系。

English

What we collect

Nothing. This app requires no account, contains no advertising SDK, uses no analytics or third-party tracking services, and uses no cloud services. All detection data (images, network scan results, sensor readings) is processed only in the local memory of your device and is never uploaded or saved to any server.

How data is stored

This app does not store images or scan data on disk. A list of devices you voluntarily mark as "my devices" is saved only on your device (in the app's internal storage) and is used solely to exclude known devices on future scans. It is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions

Camera: used for IR detection and lens reflection scanning. It is active only while you are on the detection screen; images are analyzed locally in real time and are never recorded or uploaded.
Local Network: WiFi scanning needs to enumerate devices on your current LAN; results are only shown locally.
Location (Android): the OS requires the location permission to read WiFi information. Your precise location is not obtained and location is never uploaded.
Bluetooth: used only for the optional Bluetooth device scan; results are shown locally.

About detection results

Detection results are for reference only and do not constitute legal evidence. Network scanning can only find devices connected to your current WiFi; cameras that are offline or use independent power and storage cannot be detected. Please combine the results with a manual check.

Children's privacy

This app is not designed for children and does not collect any personal information from children.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, please contact us through the developer contact information shown on the App Store.

Policy updates

If this policy changes, the updated content will be shown in-app. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy. Effective date: 2026-08-10.
