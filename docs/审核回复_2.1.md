# App Review 2.1「Information Needed」答复模板

> 用法：把下面 **§1 英文答复** 整段粘贴到 App Store Connect → **解决中心（Resolution Center）**，并**以附件形式上传真机录屏**；
> 再把 **§2 Notes 字段版**（精简版）粘贴到「App 审核信息 → 备注（Notes）」。
> §3 是录屏分镜脚本，§4 是提交前的自查清单。
>
> ⚠️ **粘贴前必须替换占位符**：`<BUILD>` 换成 **App Store Connect 上这次提交显示的 build 号**（本项目的 iOS build 号已由 Xcode 构建阶段 `Auto Build Number` 自动写成 Unix 秒，**不要从 `pubspec.yaml` 抄**）。`<iOS 版本号>` 换成录屏设备的 iOS 版本。
>
> 🚨 **顺序铁律：先「回复 + 附录像」，再点重新提交。**
> Apple 官方帮助原文：被拒后可以回复 Apple **并附带附件（截图、证明文件）……直到你重新提交审核为止**
> （`until you resubmit to App Review`）。也就是说 —— **一旦点了「重新提交审核 / Resubmit」，这条对话线
> 立即关闭，之后再也补交不了录屏**：重新提交后「消息」区变成只读，没有「回复 App 审核」按钮（实测）。
> 补救只能等**下一次拒信的新消息线**，白跑一轮（1~3 天）。
> **必须带附件：纯文字回复不算。**
> 回复入口只在提交状态为「**未解决的问题 / Unresolved Issues**」（刚被拒）时出现：
> App 页顶部「**查看 App 审核问题与消息 / View App Review Issues & Messages**」→「进行中 / In Progress」
> → 该提交旁「**解决 / Resolve**」→「**回复 App 审核 / Reply to App Review**」（回复框 4000 字符，下方 **Attach File**）。

## §1 解决中心答复全文（英文，可整段复制）

```
Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for your review and feedback. Below is the requested information. A screen
recording captured on a physical device (iOS <iOS 版本号>) is attached.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
App: Spy Assassin - Hidden Camera Detector, version 1.0 (build <BUILD>), bundle ID
com.spyassassin.app. The recording was captured on a physical iPhone running the latest
available iOS release, begins with launching the app from the Home screen, and shows the
complete typical user flow (see the outline below).

Two of the flows you listed do not exist in this app, because the app is intentionally
built with no account system and no user-generated content:
- Account registration / login / account deletion: NOT APPLICABLE. The app has no sign-up,
  no login, no account deletion, and collects no personal data. Everything is stored only
  on the device (local storage), and there is no server backend.
- User-generated content, content reporting and blocking: NOT APPLICABLE. There is no feed,
  no comments, no messaging, no social or shared content of any kind. The only content a
  user can produce is a private, on-device inspection report plus optional photos they
  choose from their own library; nothing is uploaded or shared inside the app.
- Paid content: SHOWN. The app's paid feature is the "Spy Assassin Pro" auto-renewable
  subscription, offered in three terms (weekly / monthly / yearly) from one subscription
  group. The app uses a "peek" model, so the free tier is intentionally limited:
  (a) FREE INCLUDES ONE COMPLETE SCAN: the free user can run one complete detection scan
      (from the guided Room Check / one-tap scan, or from a single tool) and sees the
      verdict (red / green and the risk count).
  (b) FULL RESULT DETAILS ARE LOCKED FOR FREE USERS: opening the evidence/detail list shows
      an "Upgrade to Pro" placeholder instead of the device-by-device findings.
  (c) PDF EXPORT IS LOCKED FOR FREE USERS: tapping "Export PDF" on the report screen opens
      the paywall instead of exporting.
  (d) STARTING ANOTHER SCAN AFTER THE FREE ONE IS LOCKED: the free quota is one scan; a
      second scan attempt opens the paywall.
  (e) SCAN HISTORY: the free tier keeps the most recent 5 records; Pro keeps unlimited.
  The recording demonstrates (a)-(e) and then opens the paywall from the More tab ->
  "Spy Assassin Pro" card, which displays all three plans, their prices, the auto-renewal
  terms, "Restore Purchases", the Terms of Use (EULA) link and the Privacy Policy link.
  Tapping a plan opens the standard StoreKit purchase sheet, which is then cancelled
  (no purchase is completed).

Recording outline:
- Launching the app from the Home screen; Home hub with the privacy banner.
- Guided "Room Check": the 4-step checklist (visual check -> IR -> network -> magnet/BLE).
- IR Detection: camera permission prompt, live IR sweep view, and the in-app explanation of
  what can and cannot be detected.
- Lens Reflection Scan (More -> Lens reflection scan): camera-based reflected-light sweep.
- WiFi Network Scan: local-network permission prompt, scan, device list, tagging a device.
- Magnet Detector: live magnetometer reading and vibration alert near a magnet.
- Bluetooth Scan and Tracker Scan: Bluetooth permission prompt, nearby BLE devices,
  including AirTag/SmartTag-style trackers.
- Inspection report: add a photo, generate the report, then tap "Export as PDF" — for the
  free user this opens the paywall (demonstrating that PDF export is a paid feature).
- Scan History: list of saved scans (free keeps the last 5).
- Free-scan limit: after the first scan is used, starting another scan opens the paywall.
- Locked details: expanding the result details / evidence list shows the "Upgrade to Pro"
  placeholder for a free user.
- More tab -> Spy Assassin Pro -> paywall: weekly, monthly and yearly plans, prices,
  renewal terms, Restore Purchases, Terms of Use (EULA), Privacy Policy (tapping a plan
  opens the standard StoreKit sheet, which is then cancelled).
- Settings: language switch (English / Simplified Chinese / Korean / Spanish) and
  "delete local data".

2. APP PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Spy Assassin is a fully offline privacy utility that helps people check hotel rooms, rental
apartments, changing rooms and bathrooms for hidden cameras and for Bluetooth trackers that
may be following them. It solves a real, widely reported privacy problem (hidden-camera
incidents in hotels and short-term rentals) by turning several hard-to-use detection
techniques into one guided, about-4-minute room check.

Target audience: travelers and business travelers (especially women aged 25-40) and anyone
staying in hotels, homestays or short-term rentals; secondarily, privacy-conscious people at
home. The value is trust through honesty: no account, no ads, no cloud upload, no data
collection; detection runs entirely on the device; and the app clearly states what it can
and cannot detect instead of over-claiming. Findings can be summarized in a PDF report for
documentation. Results are presented as reference only, not as legal evidence.

3. SETUP AND HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, no credentials, no sample files and no configuration are required. Steps:
a) Install and launch the app. There is no account creation and no sign-in of any kind.
b) Permissions are requested only when the corresponding tool is opened, and each is used
   strictly on-device:
   - Camera - used by IR Detection and Lens Reflection Scan.
   - Local Network - used by the WiFi Network Scan.
   - Bluetooth - used by the Bluetooth Scan and Tracker Scan.
   - Photo Library - only to attach a user-chosen photo to a report.
   - Motion sensors (magnetometer) require no permission prompt.
c) To reproduce detection results:
   - IR Detection works in a dark room: switch off the lights and sweep the IR Detection
     view slowly across the room. Infrared emitting sources (e.g. an IR remote control or a
     phone's front camera/TrueDepth sensor) appear as bright spots.
   - Lens Reflection Scan: in a dim room, hold the phone's flashlight on the same axis as
     the object and look for a bright lens reflection.
   - WiFi Network Scan: connect the iPhone to the same Wi-Fi network as the devices to be
     checked; the app then lists LAN devices and fingerprints camera-like RTSP/HTTP
     services.
   - Magnet Detector: move the top-right area of the iPhone (sensor location) slowly over
     suspicious objects; a magnet produces a high reading and a vibration alert.
   - Bluetooth / Tracker Scan: stand still for a few seconds; nearby BLE devices and
     AirTag/SmartTag-style trackers are listed.
d) Report flow: open the report screen, optionally add a photo from the library, generate
   the report and tap "Export as PDF". For a free user the export is gated by the Pro
   paywall; subscribing (or using a sandbox/test account that is already Pro) unlocks the
   export, which then opens the iOS share sheet.
e) Demo account: not applicable - the app has no accounts. The paid features can be
   reviewed without purchasing by reading the paywall; no purchase is needed to review.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
Core functionality runs entirely on-device, offline. There is no server backend, no user
database and no data transmission from the app.
- Payment: Apple StoreKit only (in-app purchase). Products:
  com.spyassassin.app.pro.weekly1 (auto-renewable weekly),
  com.spyassassin.app.pro.monthly2 (auto-renewable monthly) and
  com.spyassassin.app.pro.yearly2 (auto-renewable yearly), all in one subscription group
  and submitted with this version.
- Authentication services: none.
- Data providers: none.
- AI services: none in this version.
- Analytics / advertising / attribution / crash-reporting SDKs: none.
- Cloud storage / CDN: none for app data. Only the static Privacy Policy and support pages
  are plain HTML hosted on GitHub Pages and opened in Safari through a link; they collect no
  data.
- Open-source components: Flutter framework and standard open-source packages (camera,
  sensors, Bluetooth, networking, PDF generation) and the bundled Noto Sans SC / Noto Sans
  KR fonts, used under the SIL Open Font License. The camera-spot detection kernel is our
  own C code compiled through Flutter FFI. No protected third-party material is included.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app is functionally identical in every region and storefront; no feature and no
content is limited by region, and no region is excluded. The interface is localized into
English, Simplified Chinese, Korean and Spanish based on the device language. The only
difference between storefronts is the subscription price displayed by Apple according to its
per-storefront price tiers.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. Spy Assassin is a general consumer privacy utility. It does not operate in a
regulated industry, does not offer regulated services, and does not include protected
third-party material. All detection algorithms and all app text are original. Third-party
product names (e.g. AirTag, SmartTag) appear only as descriptive text for what the app may
detect; the app is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any hardware brand. The
app states that results are for reference only and are not legal evidence.

Thank you for your time. Please let us know if any additional information is needed.
```

## §3 录屏分镜脚本（在真机 iPhone 上录，必须从「点图标启动」开始）

> 要求：真机 + 最新 iOS；**开头必须是主屏点 App 图标启动**；一次录完，可用 iOS 自带录屏（控制中心里的录制按钮）。建议先插上电源、开飞行模式以外的正常状态，避免中途弹通知。
> **重要：录屏时必须是「免费（未订阅）」状态**，否则看不到付费墙的门控（第 8、10、11 步）。若你的设备已是 Pro，请先删 App 重装，或到「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」确认没有生效中的订阅。

1. 主屏 → 点 App 图标启动 → 首页（顶部展示「无账号 · 无广告 · 无云上传 · 数据不落盘」条）
2. 首页「房间排查 / Room Check」→ 展示 4 步清单（目视 → 红外 → 网络 → 磁力/蓝牙）
3. **红外检测**：进入 → 相机权限弹窗（点允许）→ 关灯/弱光下缓慢扫动 → 展示「能检测 / 不能检测」说明
4. 返回「更多」→ **镜头反光扫描** → 手电筒照向物体
5. **WiFi 扫描**：进入 → 本地网络权限弹窗（点允许）→ 开始扫描 → 设备列表 → 给一台设备打标签
6. **磁力计**：进入 → 把带磁物体靠近手机右上角（振动/读数升高）
7. **蓝牙扫描 / 防跟踪扫描**：进入 → 蓝牙权限弹窗（点允许）→ 附近设备列表
8. **一键扫描出结论（消耗那 1 次免费扫描）**：首页「一键扫描」/ 或任一工具跑一次 → 得到红/绿结论页
   - **展开「查看证据明细」→ 免费用户显示「升级 Pro 查看完整检测结果与设备明细」占位**（证明明细被锁）
9. **检测报告**：新建报告 → 从相册加一张照片 → 生成报告 → **点「导出 PDF」→ 弹出付费墙**（证明 PDF 导出被锁）→ 关掉付费墙
10. **再发起一次扫描** → 弹出付费墙（`免费扫描次数已用完`，证明免费额度=1 次）→ 关掉
11. **扫描历史**：展示列表与「免费保留最近 5 份」提示
12. **付费墙**：更多 → 点「Spy Assassin Pro」卡片 → 付费墙（**周/月/年三档**、价格、自动续费说明、恢复购买、使用条款、隐私政策）→ 点套餐唤起 StoreKit 购买弹窗 → **取消**（不要真付款）
13. **设置**：切换语言（英/中/韩/西）与「清除本地数据」
14. 结束

录制要点：
- 全程英文界面建议（审核员读英文），可在设置里切到 English 再录。
- 相机/本地网络/蓝牙权限弹窗都要出现在录屏里，证明权限是按需申请的。
- **付费门控（第 8、9、10、11 步）是本次 2.1 问询的重点，务必录到**：明细被锁、PDF 被锁、免费次数用完、历史 5 份。这样「付费内容」与商店描述、与答复文字三者一致。
- 不要录到任何个人隐私信息（相册里的私人照片、Wi-Fi 名称里的个人信息等）。
- 视频建议 ≤ 5 分钟、控制在几百 MB 内，直接上传到解决中心附件。

## §4 提交前自查（苹果邮件里「Prevent Common Issues」对应项）

- [ ] **占位符已替换**：§1/§2 里的 `<BUILD>` 用 **ASC 上这次提交显示的 build 号**（不要抄 `pubspec.yaml`）；`<iOS 版本号>` 用录屏设备的版本。
- [ ] **3.1.1 内购**：`com.spyassassin.app.pro.weekly1` / `.monthly2` / `.yearly2` **三个订阅**均处于「准备提交 / 已批准」，并且**已添加进本次 App 版本**（版本页「App 内购买项目」区勾选/添加），否则审核员点付费墙看不到价格。
- [ ] **3.1.2 订阅合规**：付费墙内价格、周期（周/月/年）、自动续费与取消说明、恢复购买、使用条款(EULA)、隐私政策链接齐全（代码已内置，勿删）；付费墙**不默认勾选**任何套餐属于加分项，本项目默认选中「周度」，如被问询可解释为默认聚焦档位但用户可自由切换。
- [ ] **2.3.3 截图**：商店截图是真实 App 界面（现有 7 张，非启动页/标题图）。
- [ ] **2.1 一致性（最重要）**：商店描述、Notes、答复文字、App 实际行为**四者一致**——免费= 1 次扫描 + 结论，完整明细 / PDF / 无限历史需 Pro。若商店描述仍写「全部工具免费、PDF 免费」，会二次被拒。
- [ ] **2.1 崩溃**：真机（iPhone XS Max）完整走一遍 §3 全部流程无崩溃；有条件先过 TestFlight。
- [ ] **隐私政策 URL** 可公网访问：`https://roma007.github.io/spy-assassin/privacy.html`
- [ ] **附录像**：解决中心回复需要**附件**，纯文字回复不算。
- [ ] 回复后不要直接「重新提交新版本」；先在解决中心答复 → 按苹果要求（通常同一版本可继续审核，或需重新提交）。
- [ ] 重新提交时**上传新 build**（build 号已自动递增，无需手动改）。
